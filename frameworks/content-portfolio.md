# Content Portfolio

A strategy should allocate scarce production capacity across different jobs.

### Core / compounding
Repeatable work that builds durable authority, search surface area, education, or conversion support.

### Responsive / fresh
Time-sensitive opportunities supported by fresh signals plus audience/business relevance.

### Experiments
Uncertain bets with an explicit hypothesis and evaluation method.

### Deferred
Potentially useful, but blocked by timing, evidence, resources, or lower opportunity cost.

### Rejected / not now
Explicitly excluded so the strategy has real trade-offs.

## Allocation

No universal percentage is required. Allocation must reflect objective, lifecycle stage, channel dynamics, and production capacity.

The strategist should explain why the chosen mix fits current conditions.
