# Evidence Lineage

## Chain

```text
RESEARCH SOURCE
→ RESEARCH CLAIM
→ RESEARCH INSIGHT / MARKET OPPORTUNITY / VOC / SIGNAL
→ CONTENT OPPORTUNITY
→ STRATEGY DECISION
→ CREATOR BRIEF
→ PUBLISHED CONTENT
→ PERFORMANCE OBSERVATION
→ STRATEGY UPDATE
```

## Rules

1. Keep upstream IDs intact.
2. Do not cite a raw source directly when a verified upstream claim already exists unless additional context is required.
3. A content opportunity should expose both supporting and contradicting evidence IDs.
4. A strategy decision based primarily on hypotheses must include validation.
5. Creator briefs inherit the evidence requirements and forbidden unsupported claims from the strategy decision.
