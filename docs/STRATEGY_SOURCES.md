# Strategy Source Audit

Research baseline: 2026-09-17.

This project does not treat GitHub popularity, a single testimonial, or an author's own case study as proof of business impact.

## Evidence dimensions

Each upstream source is evaluated separately on:

1. **adoption** — observable repository/community usage signals;
2. **independent usage** — evidence that people other than the author use/test it;
3. **result evidence** — whether outcomes are reported and by whom;
4. **replication strength** — whether comparable outcomes have been independently repeated;
5. **mechanism quality** — whether the strategic logic is explicit, inspectable, falsifiable, and adaptable.

## Sources currently used as pattern inputs

### coreyhaines31/marketingskills

Source: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills

Used for mechanisms including:
- load product/business/customer context before strategy;
- separate content planning from copywriting;
- content portfolio thinking;
- customer/content-market fit prioritization;
- searchable vs shareable content jobs;
- experiment and measurement discipline.

Evidence interpretation:
- strong public adoption and independent usage signals;
- mature and detailed strategic decomposition;
- public adoption is **not** treated as proof that the exact content scoring weights cause revenue or reach.

### social-media-skills/skills

Source: https://github.com/social-media-skills/skills

Used for:
- explicit strategic trade-offs;
- selecting channels by audience/format/goal/capacity fit;
- naming channels or actions that will deliberately not be pursued.

Evidence interpretation:
- useful architecture;
- newer project with weaker independent outcome evidence;
- treated as a pattern source, not authority.

### charlie947/social-media-skills

Source: https://github.com/charlie947/social-media-skills

Used for:
- compare content against the creator's own historical top/bottom performers;
- separate measured history from editorial judgement;
- extract recurring hook/topic/format/CTA patterns from first-party data.

Evidence interpretation:
- author has published strong first-party outcome claims;
- independent replication of those outcomes is not yet strong enough to treat the tactics as universal;
- the reusable mechanism is **relative first-party learning**, not the author's exact formulas.

### imMamdouhaboammar/marketing-council-pack

Source: https://github.com/imMamdouhaboammar/marketing-council-pack

Used for:
- decision ownership before tactics;
- evidence gates;
- skeptical counterweights;
- explicit alternatives, measurement, and reversal criteria;
- bounded routing instead of running every marketing framework at once.

Evidence interpretation:
- sophisticated, inspectable architecture;
- public evidence of repeated downstream business outcomes remains limited;
- use as governance/reasoning mechanism, not a promise of growth.

## Rule for future additions

A new external pattern must record:

```text
source
mechanism
adoption evidence
independent usage evidence
result evidence
limitations
transfer assumptions
```

If result evidence comes only from the author, mark it as such.

If independent business outcomes are not available, say so explicitly.

No pattern receives authority merely because it is famous, highly starred, or frequently repeated in marketing advice.
