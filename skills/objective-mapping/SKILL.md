# Skill: Objective Mapping

## Mission
Translate the business objective into content objectives and observable indicators.

## Rules
- choose a primary objective and at most a small number of secondary objectives;
- distinguish business outcome, content outcome, and diagnostic platform metrics;
- do not optimize for reach/followers by default;
- avoid invented numeric targets when no baseline exists.

## Output
`primary_objective`, `secondary_objectives`, `content_jobs`, `leading_indicators`, `lagging_indicators`, `measurement_gaps`.
