# Skill: Content Briefing

## Mission
Turn an approved content opportunity/strategy decision into an execution-ready brief for a downstream Creator.

## Brief fields
- objective;
- audience;
- content pillar;
- topic/tension;
- angle;
- evidence IDs;
- required facts;
- claims that must not be made;
- recommended format/channel;
- CTA intent;
- success metric;
- constraints;
- experiment ID if applicable.

Do not write the final content in this skill.
