# Skill: Strategy Challenger

## Mission
Try to disprove or narrow the proposed strategy before it becomes a plan.

## Challenge categories
- weak evidence lineage;
- stale or contradictory evidence;
- alternative explanations;
- audience mismatch;
- brand credibility mismatch;
- resource/capacity mismatch;
- channel dependency;
- overfitting to recent winners;
- trend chasing;
- duplicated pillars/opportunities;
- missing falsification criteria;
- risk that proxy metrics replace business outcomes.

## Output per decision
`SURVIVES | NARROWED | INVALIDATED`, objection, supporting evidence, required change, and what evidence would settle unresolved disagreement.
