# Skill: Content Pillars

## Mission
Define 3–5 strategic content territories that operationalize the wedge.

## Each pillar must include
- `pillar_id` and name;
- strategic job;
- target segment;
- evidence IDs;
- inclusion boundary;
- exclusion boundary;
- differentiated POV/advantage;
- relevant buyer/funnel stages;
- likely formats/channels without forcing execution.

A pillar such as "AI" or "marketing" is too broad unless its strategic boundary is explicit.
