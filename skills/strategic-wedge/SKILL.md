# Skill: Strategic Wedge

## Mission
Define the differentiated way the brand should win attention and trust in the chosen content territory.

## Inputs
Audience evidence, competitor/market gaps, brand credibility, capabilities, business objective.

## Method
Generate candidate wedges and test each for:
1. audience relevance;
2. evidence of whitespace/differentiation;
3. credibility/right-to-speak;
4. repeatability;
5. distribution/format fit;
6. risks and obvious counter-positioning.

## Output
Selected wedge, rejected alternatives, supporting evidence IDs, confidence, and explicit `will_not_compete_on` list.
