# Skill: Strategy Synthesis

## Mission
Compile the accepted and challenged decisions into one strategy artifact.

## Required sections
- scope/date/version;
- research package and integrity;
- objective mapping;
- wedge;
- pillars;
- prioritized portfolio;
- approved content opportunities;
- experiments;
- defer/reject list;
- Creator briefs;
- challenger findings;
- research requests;
- feedback plan;
- integrity status.

Do not hide unresolved gaps for narrative neatness.
