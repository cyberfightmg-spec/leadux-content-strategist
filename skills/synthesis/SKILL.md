# Skill: Strategy Synthesis

## Mission
Compile the final strategy around the **selected Strategic Thesis**.

Do not present pillars or a calendar as the strategy itself.

## Required sections
1. scope/date/version;
2. research package and integrity;
3. relevant identity/business/context status;
4. primary objective;
5. **Strategy Diagnosis** — decisive facts, tensions, constraints, uncertainties;
6. **Strategic Options** — all materially distinct theses including status quo;
7. **Selected Strategic Thesis**;
8. strongest alternative and why it was not selected;
9. hard-gate results;
10. explicit trade-offs / `will_not_do`;
11. strategic bets and critical assumptions;
12. Strategy Validation / Challenger result;
13. reversal triggers;
14. derived strategic wedge;
15. content pillars linked to `thesis_id`;
16. prioritized portfolio and opportunities linked to `thesis_id`;
17. experiments;
18. deferred/rejected work;
19. Creator briefs;
20. measurement and feedback plan;
21. research/context requests;
22. integrity status.

## Coherence requirement
Every major downstream element must answer:

```text
THIS EXISTS BECAUSE OF THESIS [thesis_id]
```

If it cannot, flag it as generic, weakly linked, or contradictory rather than hiding the gap.

## Final honesty
Preserve:
- unresolved assumptions;
- unsupported outcomes;
- challenger objections;
- strongest alternative;
- conditions that would reverse the strategy.

Do not turn uncertainty into confidence for narrative neatness.
