# Skill: Portfolio Prioritization

## Mission
Choose what to do, test, defer and reject under real capacity constraints.

## Method
1. evaluate visible opportunity dimensions;
2. optionally calculate a transparent sortable score;
3. account for strategic dependencies and content cannibalization;
4. enforce capacity limits;
5. allocate candidates to core, responsive, experiment, deferred or rejected buckets.

## Required output
Prioritized portfolio plus reasons for every deferred/rejected high-interest candidate.
